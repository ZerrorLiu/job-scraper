# Profile notes

Facts about you that the tailoring agent may draw on when it rewrites a resume
for one job. It may re-emphasize what is here; it may not go beyond it.

Write plainly and only what you can defend in an interview. Anything vague here
becomes a vague claim on a resume you have to stand behind.

## Background

One paragraph: what you have actually built and operated, and in what setting.

## Strengths

- A thing you are genuinely good at, and where that shows.
- Another, different in kind from the first.

## Constraints

- Work authorization, location, notice period, languages -- whatever a hiring
  manager would need to know early.
